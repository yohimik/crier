# The anthem

`anthem.mp3` is sixteen seconds of the 1812 Overture finale: the
bells-and-brass climax, faded in and out, loudness-normalised.

- Source: the United States Marine Band's recording, a work of the US
  federal government and public domain outright.
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1812_Overture_-_United_States_Marine_Band.opus>
- Trim: 678s to 694s of the 12m35s performance.
- The composition is Tchaikovsky, 1880; the recording carries a Public
  Domain Mark on Wikimedia Commons.

The release's video story carries it as the soundtrack. No platform's
public API can name a licensed track, which is how a hundred-year-old
cannon barrage came to announce software; see docs/publishing/music.md.
